My bread didn't rise

✓Often rescuable

Right now

  1. Give it more time somewhere warm — cold dough is slow, not dead.
  2. Test the yeast: warm water, a pinch of sugar, 10 minutes. Foam means it's alive.
  3. If the yeast is dead, the dough can be re-kneaded with fresh yeast dissolved in a little water.

Why it happened

Yeast killed by water above ~50°C, or by direct contact with salt, or simply expired. Cold kitchens slow it dramatically but don't kill it.

Next time

Water at blood heat, salt and yeast on opposite sides of the bowl, and check the date on the packet.
