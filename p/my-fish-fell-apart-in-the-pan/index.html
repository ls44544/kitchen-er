My fish fell apart in the pan

~Repurposable

Right now

  1. Serve it as flakes over something — a salad, rice bowl or pasta. Nobody minds.
  2. Stop turning it; one flip only.

Why it happened

Fish has very little connective tissue, so cooked flesh has almost nothing holding it together. Moving it early, before the crust sets, tears it.

Next time

Dry surface, hot pan, place it skin-side down and leave it completely alone until it releases on its own.
