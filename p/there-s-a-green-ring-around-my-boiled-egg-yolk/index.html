There's a green ring around my boiled egg yolk

✗Harmless

Right now

  1. Harmless. Tastes very slightly sulphurous but it's safe.
  2. Shorter cook and an ice bath next time.

Why it happened

Sulphur in the white reacts with iron in the yolk to form iron sulphide. It only happens with prolonged heat, so it's a reliable sign of overcooking.

Next time

Boil no more than 9–10 minutes for hard, then straight into ice water to stop carryover cooking.
