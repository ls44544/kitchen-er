My guacamole / apple went brown

✗Cosmetic

Right now

  1. Scrape off the brown layer — the flesh underneath is fine.
  2. Stir in lime juice and press cling film directly onto the surface.

Why it happened

An enzyme reacts with oxygen and turns phenols into brown pigment. It's harmless and doesn't affect flavour much.

Next time

Acid slows the enzyme and excluding air stops it. Contact-wrap the surface rather than covering the bowl.
