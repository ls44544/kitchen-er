My sauce is too salty

✓Usually rescuable

Right now

  1. Dilute: add unsalted stock, water, cream or crushed tomatoes.
  2. Add bulk: more vegetables, beans, rice or pasta will absorb it.
  3. Add acid and a pinch of sugar — lemon or vinegar makes salt read as less harsh.

Why it happened

Salt doesn't evaporate. As liquid reduces, concentration climbs — a sauce seasoned correctly at the start is often too salty after 30 minutes of reduction.

Next time

Season at the end of reduction, not the start. Especially with stock cubes, soy or anchovy already in the pot.
