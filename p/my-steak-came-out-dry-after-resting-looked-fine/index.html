My steak came out dry after resting looked fine

~Partly rescuable

Right now

  1. Pour the board juices back over the sliced meat.
  2. Serve with butter, a pan sauce or a compound butter.

Why it happened

Heat drives moisture toward the cooler centre and tightens the fibres. Cut immediately and that pressure dumps onto the board. Rest, and much of it redistributes.

Next time

Rest roughly half the cooking time, somewhere warm, loosely covered. A thick steak wants 8–10 minutes.
