My scrambled eggs are watery

✗Not rescuable

Right now

  1. Pour off the liquid and serve immediately — it will only get worse.
  2. Next batch: lower heat and stop cooking while they still look underdone.

Why it happened

Overcooked egg protein contracts and squeezes out the water it was holding. The pool in the pan is water wrung out of the curds. Salting long before cooking accelerates it.

Next time

Medium-low heat, constant movement, off the heat when they're still glossy and slightly loose. Salt just before serving.
