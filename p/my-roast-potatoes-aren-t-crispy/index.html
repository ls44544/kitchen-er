My roast potatoes aren't crispy

✓Rescuable

Right now

  1. Crank the oven to 220°C, spread them out with space between, and give them 15 more minutes.
  2. Don't add more oil — crowding, not oil, is usually the problem.

Why it happened

Crisping needs surface water gone and space for steam to escape. A crowded tray becomes a steam box.

Next time

Parboil, rough up the edges, dry them off, hot fat, and one layer with gaps.
