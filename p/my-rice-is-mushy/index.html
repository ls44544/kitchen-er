My rice is mushy

~Partly rescuable

Right now

  1. Take the lid off and put it on low heat for a few minutes to steam off moisture.
  2. Spread on a tray to cool — it firms up considerably.
  3. Or accept it and make fried rice tomorrow; day-old mushy rice fries well.

Why it happened

Too much water, or stirring, which abrades starch off the grains. Once the grain wall bursts, texture is gone for good.

Next time

1:1.5 rice to water for long grain, lid on, no stirring, and rest off the heat for 10 minutes before fluffing.
