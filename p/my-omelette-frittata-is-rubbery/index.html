My omelette / frittata is rubbery

✗Not rescuable

Right now

  1. No fix once set. Serve with something saucy to mask it.
  2. Cut heat and time by a third next attempt.

Why it happened

Egg proteins set from about 62°C and tighten sharply above 70°C. Too hot a pan sets the outside long before the middle, so by the time the centre cooks the edge is leather.

Next time

Moderate heat and pull it while the top is still slightly wet. Carryover heat finishes it on the plate.
