My cheese sauce went stringy and oily

✓Rescuable

Right now

  1. Off the heat, whisk in a splash of evaporated milk or a cornflour slurry.
  2. A teaspoon of lemon juice or sodium citrate will pull it back smooth.

Why it happened

Too much heat makes cheese proteins clump and squeeze out their fat. Aged hard cheeses split most easily because they hold less moisture.

Next time

Add grated cheese off the heat, a handful at a time, and never let it boil.
