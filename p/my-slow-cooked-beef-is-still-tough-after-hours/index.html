My slow-cooked beef is still tough after hours

✓Time fixes it

Right now

  1. Keep going. It genuinely needs more time — another hour, sometimes two.
  2. Make sure it's actually at a simmer and the meat is mostly submerged.

Why it happened

Collagen converts to gelatine slowly. Meat passes through a tough stage on the way to tender, and stopping there is the single most common braising mistake. It is not overcooked — it's early.

Next time

Cook to texture, never to the clock. A fork should twist freely with no resistance.
